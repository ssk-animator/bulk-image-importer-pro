Privacy Policy — Bulk Image Importer Pro

Last updated: October 2026.

What this add-in does

Bulk Image Importer Pro runs locally in your Excel session. Images you select are read from your device and inserted into your workbook. The add-in does not upload your images, workbooks, or personal data to any server operated by the developer.

Data stored

  • Add-in settings (layout defaults, theme) are stored locally in your browser/Office environment only.
  • No account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising identifiers are collected by the add-in.

Third parties

The add-in loads the official Microsoft Office.js library and is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Standard web-server logs (e.g., IP address, requested file) may be processed by those infrastructure providers under their own policies.
